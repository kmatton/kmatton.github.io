I am an incoming PhD student in the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory ([CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/)) at MIT. I am currently working as a research assistant at the University of Michigan, where I completed both my M.S.E. and B.S.E. in computer science. During my time at U of M, I have been fortunate to be advised by Professor [Emily Mower Provost](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~emilykmp/). 

My research is focused on designing machine learning approaches to support human health and well-being. 
I am particulary interested in modeling the relationship between everyday behavior and well-being. I work on algorithms that can extract actionable insights from complex, "in the wild" data, such as the signals produced by mobile phones and smartwatches. Most of my [prior work](publications.md) has focused on methods of automatically monitoring symptom severity in individuals with Bipolar Disorder using everyday smartphone conversations.

You can contact me at kmatton@mit.edu.
