Hi! I'm a PhD student in computer science at MIT, advised by [John Guttag](https://people.csail.mit.edu/guttag/) and [Roz Picard](https://www.media.mit.edu/people/picard/overview/). My research interests span machine learning, causality, interpretability, and self-supervised representation learning. My work is motivated by the goal of making machine learning models safer and more reliable for use in high-stakes settings; in particular, I am interested in healthcare and mental healthcare applications.

I received my M.S.E. and B.S.E. degrees in computer science from the University of Michigan in 2019 and 2018, repsectively. While there, I worked with [Emily Mower Provost](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~emilykmp/) on machine learning methods for detecting symptom severity in individuals with bipolar disorder.
